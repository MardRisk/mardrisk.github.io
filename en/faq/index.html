Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to the most common questions about MardRisk AI.

What is MardRisk 1.1?

MardRisk 1.1 is the first financial LLM. It answers any financial question with live charts, real-time market data, accurate calculations, 1000-scenario simulations via multi-agent system, and MardRisk Shield file forensics — in perfect Armenian, English or Russian.

How do I use it?

Open ai.mardriskai.online (or the Telegram bot). Type any financial question, ask for simulations, or upload documents (PDF/Excel) for Shield analysis. Switch languages anytime.

Is core access free?

The full power of 1.1 (chat, live data, simulations, Shield) is available through the app. See academy.mardrisk.online to learn how to get the most from it.

Does it store my financial data?

Our product is designed to minimize data storage. For more detail, please review the privacy policy.

Is this financial advice?

No. MardRisk AI provides informational support and risk guidance, but it is not a substitute for licensed financial, tax, legal, or investment advice.

Which languages are supported?

The site and service support English, Armenian, and Russian.

How can I contact support?

You can contact us by email at info@mardriskai.online or through Telegram at @MardRiskAI_bot.
