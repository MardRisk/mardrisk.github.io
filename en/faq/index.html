Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to the most common questions about MardRisk AI.

What is MardRisk AI?

MardRisk AI is an AI-powered assistant that helps users better understand financial risk, spending pressure, and decision tradeoffs through fast, simple conversations.

How do I use it?

You can use MardRisk AI through the Telegram bot or supported web interfaces. Share your question, purchase idea, budget concern, or financial context, and the service will return guidance.

Is it free?

Core access may be available for free, while some advanced or premium features may require payment. The pricing page will always show what is included.

Does it store my financial data?

Our product is designed to minimize data storage. For more detail, please review the privacy policy.

Is this financial advice?

No. MardRisk AI provides informational support and risk guidance, but it is not a substitute for licensed financial, tax, legal, or investment advice.

Which languages are supported?

The site and service support English, Armenian, and Russian.

How can I contact support?

You can contact us by email at info@mardriskai.online or through Telegram at @MardRiskAI_bot.
