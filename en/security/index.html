Trust & Security

Security
by design

Last updated: 2026

Privacy by design

MardRisk 1.1 is built so that we do not store your financial data. The service analyzes your question or uploaded file in real time and returns the result without keeping your data on our servers.

What we do not collect

  • Detailed financial account information
  • Banking or payment details
  • Conversation history for storage purposes
  • Personally identifiable information

MardRisk Shield & file security

Uploaded files are processed only for analysis and are not stored long-term. Files are deleted after analysis. We never use your data to train our models.

Technical protections

All connections are protected with HTTPS/TLS. Our multi-agent system cross-verifies answers to reduce the risk of errors and hallucinations.

Transparency

We publicly state that we do not sell or share your data with third parties. Full details are available in our Privacy Policy.
