About MardRisk AI

financial companion.

MardRisk AI helps people understand financial risk before they spend, borrow, or make a money decision.

The Product

What MardRisk AI is.

MardRisk AI is a financial assistant focused on risk clarity. Instead of only showing expenses or generic advice, it explains how risky a user's financial situation is and why a purchase, loan, or habit may become dangerous.

📊
Risk Score

A fast personal financial risk score based on income, spending pressure, debt, and financial stability — one clear number that shows where you stand.

🛒
Purchase Check

Evaluate whether a purchase is safe, risky, or better postponed according to your real situation, not generic rules or averages.

🏦
Loan Intelligence

Analyze loans, debt pressure, and repayment burden, then get clearer recommendations tailored to your actual financial capacity.

🌍
Multilingual AI

Designed to work in English, Armenian, and Russian so more users can understand financial risk in their own language and context.
