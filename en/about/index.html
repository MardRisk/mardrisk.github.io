What MardRisk 1.1 is.

MardRisk 1.1 is the financial LLM that delivers precise answers, live visualizations, real computations, multi-agent reasoning, 1000-scenario simulations, and forensic analysis of uploaded financial files — with flawless support for three languages.

🧠
Financial LLM

Ask any financial question. Get reasoned answers backed by live data and verifiable calculations — not generic text.

Live Charts & Data

Real-time interactive graphs and current market prices rendered directly in the conversation.

1000-Scenario Sims

Press simulate and the multi-agent engine runs 1000 paths to give probabilistic, actionable ranges.

Upload statements, reports or contracts. Detect fraud, hidden risks, forgery patterns and get a full forensic report.
